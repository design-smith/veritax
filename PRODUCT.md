# Product

## Register

product

## Stage

Stage 1 (2026–27): global company-data product for transfer-pricing professionals. Vision: `VISION.md`.

## Users

Transfer-pricing practitioners (in-house tax, Big Four, boutique advisors) who currently pay for expensive company databases to screen comparables, understand legal entities, and assemble a benchmarking universe. They work at a desk on a large monitor, in daylight or office light. They are precise, skeptical of unsourced claims, and intolerant of decorative UI.

Local File authors are the same people later. They are not the Stage 1 buyer job.

## Product Purpose

Veritax is a provenance-aware global company database, presented the way TP work actually happens.

The $90 seat promise:

> Everything a TP professional needs to understand and compare companies, in one place, already organized the way they work.

Search is the product. A practitioner starts from the full public-company universe, screens it like a benchmarking study, opens a sourced record, and can save the strategy. Trust is earned by reproducing work they already did in Orbis / Capital IQ / a Big Four study.

Success: they begin with tens of thousands of companies, see every criterion that reduced the set, and end with a candidate list they would defend. The record they open shows identity, footprint, financials, ownership, and sources — not a marketing card.

Local File generation, credits, and the rest of the workflow remain in the app as later-stage tools. They must not compete with search for the front door.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, exact, unhurried. Voice is plain and specific. No marketing cadence. Confidence comes from evidence, not adjectives.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS search consoles with wrapping filter-chip toolbars. Google SERP layouts. Identical company card grids. Hero-metric dashboards. Cream/sand AI palettes. Display fonts in app chrome. Invented widgets where a list or field would do. Americas-only defaults dressed up as a global product.

## Design Principles

1. Evidence over atmosphere. Show the fact, the source, the number. Do not decorate the absence of either.
2. One instrument per task. Search is a field plus a visible screening funnel, not a form for its own sake. Draft, when it exists, is a document, not a dashboard.
3. Deterministic beats theatrical. State changes should be readable; motion only to explain a change of mode.
4. Familiar product vocabulary (Linear, Spotlight, Stripe, Orbis). Strangeness needs a job.
5. Unknown stays unknown. Empty, error, incomplete, and non-redistributable states tell the truth.
6. The funnel stays visible. Every active criterion remains on screen. Clearing it restores the prior count.
7. Global unless the user narrows it. Region is a filter, not a default prison.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA as the floor. Body and placeholder text ≥4.5:1. Visible `:focus-visible` rings using `--color-ring`. Search CTA is white on `#111`. Keyboard path for search (arrows, Enter, Escape, `/` to focus). Results are a full-page table. The universe is visible without a query; filters reduce it. `prefers-reduced-motion` is respected. No information by color alone.
